# Managing your products (simple guide)

Open **your-website.com/admin** and press **Login with GitHub**. Click **Products** on the left.

## Add a new product
1. Press **＋ Product**.
2. **Product name:** the name customers see. Every product needs a different name.
3. **Photo:** press *Choose an image* → *Upload* → pick the photo → *Choose selected*.
   - JPG or WebP, at least 800 px wide, **under 800 KB** (large phone photos slow the site: shrink them first
     at squoosh.app).
4. **Category:** pick one from the list.
5. **Starting price (₹)** and **Base size:** the price is for the base size. Other sizes are priced in
   proportion (12 cupcakes cost double the price of 6; 3 donuts cost 3 times the price of 1).
6. **Sizes customers can choose:** tick every size you sell. Use *either* pieces (cupcakes, brownies, donuts)
   *or* weights (loose chocolates), not both.
7. **Description:** a sentence or two about the flavour and toppings.
8. Press **Publish → Publish now**.

The product appears on the website in **1–2 minutes**.

## Change a price, photo or description
Click the product in the list, change it, then **Publish → Publish now**.

## Hide a product (out of season) without deleting it
Open the product, switch **Show on website** off, publish. Switch it back on any time.

## Delete a product
Open it → **Delete entry**. (Hiding is safer: you can bring it back.)

## Where does the product appear in the list?
New products go to the end. To place one earlier, fill **Position in the list**: a smaller number is shown
first (1 = first). The existing products are numbered 10, 20, 30… so, for example, 15 puts a product between the
products at 10 and 20.

## Good to know
- Prices are whole rupees.
- Search the list with the box at the top; filter *No photo yet* or *Hidden products* from **Filter by**.
- If a change hasn't appeared after 5 minutes, tell your developer: the system may have found a mistake
  in a product and kept the old website in place so nothing breaks.
